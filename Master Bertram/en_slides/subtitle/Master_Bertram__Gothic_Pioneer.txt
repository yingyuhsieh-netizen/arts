Part 6. The Northern Legacy

[Master_Bertram__Gothic_Pioneer_slide_0023.png]
So, what's the ultimate takeaway here? What is Master Bertram's legacy? He might not be a
household name like the Italian masters, but his impact on Northern European art was
absolutely profound. No, he didn't trigger a classical renaissance like Giotto did,
but he established something just as crucial for his region, a thriving, highly collaborative,
artistic tradition. His workshop system paved the way for brilliant successors,
creating a really robust tradition of high-quality religious art in Hamburg.
He proved, beyond a shadow of a doubt, that a Northern mercantile city could produce
monumental, emotionally resonant art that perfectly captured that hardworking,
practical, yet deeply faithful, Hanseatic spirit.
